This Agreement protects confidential business discussions and proprietary relationships. The complete downloadable agreement controls. The text below is a readable web copy for review and printing.

1. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” includes all non-public information disclosed or made available in any form, including business plans, finances, budgets, pricing, ownership, investors, deal terms, customer and partner relationships, contacts, opportunities, locations, properties, hospitality, food, event, retail, service, app, technology, media, product, intellectual-property, brand, marketing, operational and expansion strategies, source code, software, prompts, databases, workflows, automations, APIs, credentials, security procedures, designs, graphics, logos, decks, photographs, video, copy, names, concepts, recipes, formulas, research, prototypes, product plans, event plans, personnel information, and materials derived from them.

2. Confidentiality and Restricted Use

The receiving party shall protect Confidential Information using at least reasonable care, use it only for the authorized purpose, disclose it only to representatives who need to know and are bound by protective obligations, avoid unauthorized publicity, maintain reasonable security, and promptly notify the disclosing party of any compromise. Confidential Information may not be used to compete with, disadvantage, bypass, imitate, reverse engineer, exploit, or harm the disclosing party or its enterprise.

3. Non-Circumvention

During the Agreement and for twenty-four (24) months after the last disclosure or introduction, the receiving party shall not bypass the disclosing party concerning any protected contact or opportunity. Without written consent, it shall not independently solicit, negotiate with, contract with, invest in, acquire from, sell to, employ, finance, develop, license, or partner with a protected contact or opportunity in a manner that deprives the disclosing party of its anticipated role, economics, ownership, fee, commission, credit, access, or benefit.

4. Non-Solicitation and Non-Interference

For twenty-four (24) months after the last material interaction, neither party shall knowingly target employees or exclusive contractors of the other with whom it had material contact or about whom it received Confidential Information, except through general advertisements. Neither party shall intentionally interfere with contracts, customer relationships, vendors, financing, sponsorships, or strategic partnerships using Confidential Information.

5. Proprietary Rights

All Confidential Information and pre-existing intellectual property remain the property of the disclosing party. No license, assignment, partnership, joint venture, agency, franchise, or ownership interest is created. Unauthorized copying, recording, scraping, reproduction, distribution, reverse engineering, AI training, or derivative use is prohibited.

6. Return and Destruction

Upon written request or termination of discussions, Confidential Information shall be returned or permanently destroyed, including copies and derivatives, subject only to limited legal archival and routine backup retention that remains confidential.

7. Term

The Agreement continues for five (5) years. Confidentiality survives for five years after each disclosure. Trade-secret obligations survive for so long as the information remains a trade secret. Non-circumvention and non-solicitation survive according to their stated terms.

8. Remedies

Unauthorized use, disclosure, circumvention, solicitation, or interference may cause irreparable harm. The injured party may seek injunctive relief, specific performance, damages, attorneys’ fees and costs where permitted, and all other remedies available at law or in equity.

9. Governing Law

Georgia law governs. State and federal courts in Fulton County, Georgia have jurisdiction, subject to emergency relief being available in any court with jurisdiction over the breaching party or affected property.

10. Electronic Signatures

The Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature or transmission. Each signer represents authority to bind the identified party.
